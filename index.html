No Name Given (Mason County)

Unnamed buyers offered a Kentucky farming family $10M for their land, refusing to say who they were unless an NDA was signed first. The family said no, but eighteen neighbors said yes. The public didn’t learn a project existed there until months later, at an unrelated meeting.

amazon water · organisation

Three Years Of Silence (Amazon water)

A leaked 2022 memo shows Amazon weighing which headlines to avoid before disclosing its water use. A number finally came in 2025 — 2.5 billion gallons — wrapped in a comparison against a narrower competitor figure. Reports also described water replenishment projects as offsetting consumption instead of restoration efforts.

If the silence lasted three years, how would anyone know when the next one starts?

patterns

What These Stories Add Up To

Almost nothing here ends. A win in one place just relocates the same project somewhere else. A court order gets appealed instead of followed. An investigation answers one question and opens three more.

What's been interesting is what actually decides whether anyone responds at all — not how much harm is happening, but whether a place has the press, the legal aid, the organizing capacity to respond in the first place. That's not evenly distributed.

06 · unresolved / absence no lever activated, or too recent to know

Disclosure and usability are different problems. A number can be legally public and still functionally hidden until someone translates it.

The lever that works depends on what kind of entity you’re up against — a company, a public utility, and a university all get handled completely differently, even within the same country.

Absence of resistance isn’t absence of harm. It’s absence of the resources resistance actually requires.

Legal escalation is not a reliable lever on its own — the same tactic (a court case) produced completely different outcomes depending on what was actually enforceable once a ruling landed.
