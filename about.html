About

what this is

Real, sourced stories about what AI’s data centers cost the world around them — water, energy, land, attention. It follows on from my earlier installation, Thirsty Machines, which made AI’s hidden water costs physically tangible. This phase asks the same question in a different form: the research and numbers behind these centres are abstract, how do we make the invisible visible?

what this isn’t

Not a finished, closed set — a starting collection that could keep growing. Not original on-the-ground reporting: it is built on investigative journalism, academic papers, leaked documents and permit filings that other people gathered first. 

credits

With thanks to the journalists, researchers and local organisations whose work each of these stories rests on; the fuller source list credits them individually. It also owes a debt to other projects mapping AI’s environmental and material footprint, which shaped how this one is put together.
